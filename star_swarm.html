STAR SWARM

Slope-intercept form, point-slope form, graphs, HOY and VUX. You have 5 minutes: answer to charge your lasers, then zap the swarm.

1. AnswerEach right answer charges your lasers and earns cash. A wrong answer costs $5 and breaks your streak.
2. ZapEach shot uses 1 laser. Diving bugs are worth double, so dodge them or zap them. Stop to answer and your shield goes up.
3. UpgradeSpend cash in the Shop on more lasers, extra cannons, score multipliers and power-ups.
Keys: 1–4 answer · Enter next · A D or ← → move · W or Space fire · B shop · N music · M mute · Shift+R new game · F full screen
