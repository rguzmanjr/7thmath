SKATE

Multiplication facts, 0 to 12. You have 5 minutes. Set up with questions, then skate a line and land your tricks.

Set upAnswer 3 questions before every line. Each right answer adds ×1 to your tricks. A miss adds nothing, so don’t guess.
Drop inPress Space to drop in. You roll by yourself. Space jumps the cones, cans and benches. Ramps launch you.
TricksIn the air: ← kickflip, ↓ grab, → 360 spin. Land on a rail to grind. Finish the trick before you land.
Keys: 1–4 answer · Space or ↑ jump · ← kickflip · ↓ grab · → 360 spin · N music · M mute · Shift+R new game · F full screen
