PINBALL

Slope-intercept form. You have 5 minutes on the Burger Ball machine. Answer questions to light up your multiplier, then rack up points on the table.

1. AnswerEvery right answer lights the next multiplier lamp: 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X. Answer 2 questions to launch.
2. FlipKeep the ball alive with the flippers. Every point is multiplied. Land in the Order Up window for a bonus question and an extra ball.
3. UpgradeIf the ball drains, your multiplier resets, so answer again. Spend cash on more lamps, bigger flippers and power-ups.
Keys: 1–4 answer · Enter next question · ← → or Z / flippers · hold Space to launch
