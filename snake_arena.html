SNAKE ARENA

Slope-intercept form. You have 5 minutes: answer questions to fill your boost, then grow the longest snake in the arena.

1. Snack stopAnswer questions to fill your boost and earn cash. You stop here before every slither, and any time you press Enter.
2. SlitherEat glowing snacks to grow. Hold Space (or click) to boost. If your head hits another snake, you start small again.
3. Cut them offBoost in front of a Rival so it crashes into your body. It bursts into big snacks for you.
Keys: arrows or WASD steer (or move the mouse) · hold Space or click to boost · Enter snack stop
