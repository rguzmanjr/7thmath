Gremlin Hall

Rational number operations. Math gremlins have taken over the gym. Survive as many waves as you can. Your score is how many waves you survive.

1. SurviveMove with the arrow keys or WASD (or drag on a tablet). Your school supplies fire by themselves. Grab the gold stickers that popped gremlins drop.
2. AnswerBetween waves, every choice on the board needs a right answer: heal, level up your gear, study for stickers, or build walls and goo turrets.
3. Don’t guessA wrong answer adds 3 gremlins to the next wave, and every second wrong answer adds a tough elite gremlin.

Leaving or reloading the page during a wave ends your run. Between waves it’s safe.

Keys: arrows or WASD move · 1–4 answer · Enter continue · R rotate a wall · Esc cancel · M sound · F full screen
