BURGER SHOP

Slope-intercept form. You have 5 minutes: answer questions to stock the kitchen, then build burgers to fill the orders.

1. AnswerEach right answer earns ingredients and cash. A wrong answer costs a little cash and breaks your streak.
2. CookStack the burger from the bottom bun up to match the ticket. Each layer uses 1 ingredient. Faster is a bigger tip.
3. UpgradeSpend cash in the Supply Shop on more ingredients, bigger tips, fancy plates and power-ups.
