HILL CLIMB

Slope-intercept form. Drive your monster truck as far as you can in 5 minutes. Right answers fill your gas tank.

1. Fill upEach right answer adds gas and cash. Wrong answers add nothing, so don’t guess.
2. Drive→ gas, ← brake. In the air, brake tips the nose up and gas tips it down. Land flat on both wheels for a speed boost!
3. UpgradeSpend cash in the Garage on a bigger engine, grippier tires and more gas per answer.
Keys: → or D gas · ← or A brake · Enter gas station · S garage
