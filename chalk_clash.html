Chalk Clash

Rational number operations. Send chalk shapes down the number line and knock down the rival fort before yours falls. You have 8 minutes.

1. AnswerEach right answer gives 30 energy, plus a little more on a streak. Wrong answers give nothing, so read the hint.

3. Win roundsKnock down the rival fort to win the round. Popping shapes earns points toward evolving to 3D. Streaks charge the eraser sweep.
