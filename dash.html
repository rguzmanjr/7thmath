DASH

Multiplication facts, 0 to 12. You have 5 minutes. Right answers earn attempts and flags. How far can you dash?

1. Answer3 right answers in a row earn 1 attempt. 6 in a row earns a flag. A miss starts the count over, so don’t guess.
2. DashSpace to go. That costs 1 attempt. Space to jump spikes and climb blocks. Don’t jump under hanging spikes.
3. CheckpointPass a pole while you hold a flag and it plants itself. Crash, and you restart from your flag.
Keys: 1–4 answer · Space or ↑ go and jump (hold to keep jumping) · N music · M mute sound and music · Shift+R new game · F full screen
