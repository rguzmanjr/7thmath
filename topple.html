Topple

Rational number operations. Roll the block across the tiles and stand it up in the hole. You have 10 minutes and 10 levels.

1. AnswerEach right answer charges 8 moves. A wrong answer charges nothing and shows you why, so read it and try the next one.
2. RollArrow keys or WASD tip the block over. On a tablet, use the arrow pad or swipe. Roll off the edge and you start the level over.
3. Drop inStand the block on its end over the hole. Gold switches build a bridge after a right answer. Cracked yellow tiles break if the block stands on them.
Keys: arrows or WASD roll · 1–4 answer · Enter next question · R restart level · M sound · F full screen
